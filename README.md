# Fleet Management Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes fleet operations performance focusing on freight revenue, transportation costs, vehicle efficiency, and profitability.

The dashboard helps identify operational trends, optimize logistics costs, improve fleet utilization, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

## Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI
- DAX Measures
- Power Query
- Data Modeling
- KPI Design
- Data Visualization

---

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Freight Revenue Analysis
Key metrics analyzed:

- Net Revenue
- Goods Value
- Quantity Transported
- Weight (Ton)
- Revenue Trend Over Time
- Revenue by City
- Shipment Quantity Trend

Business Goal:
Understand revenue performance and identify top contributing locations.

---

### 2. Fleet Cost Analysis
Key metrics analyzed:

- Total Fleet Cost
- Cost per KM
- Cost per Ton
- Truck Utilization
- Fixed vs Variable Cost
- Cost Trend
- Cost by Trailer Type

Business Goal:
Monitor transportation expenses and improve cost efficiency.

---

### 3. Vehicle Performance Analysis
Key metrics analyzed:

- Profit
- Revenue
- Cost
- Gross Margin %
- Vehicle Efficiency
- Truck Performance Comparison
- Profitability by Truck Category

Business Goal:
Evaluate fleet profitability and identify high-performing vehicles.

---

## Key Insights

- Revenue changes across months indicating seasonal demand patterns.
- Some cities generate significantly higher freight revenue.
- Cost efficiency varies between trailer categories.
- Fleet utilization strongly impacts profitability.
- Vehicle performance differs across truck types.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Optimize high-cost vehicle operations.
- Increase focus on profitable routes and cities.
- Improve fleet utilization rates.
- Track Cost/KM regularly.
- Allocate trailers based on profitability.
